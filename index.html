Questions

We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before joining.

Who can join the community?

Any student who wants to learn, build, connect or contribute. You do not need to be an expert or already know how to code.

Is membership free?

Yes. The community is free to join. Individual events may have their own registration requirements.

What happens after I join?

You'll receive a welcome message, access to community updates and invitations to upcoming events, workshops and projects.

Can I volunteer or lead an event?

Absolutely. Active members can help organize sessions, mentor peers, create content and collaborate on campus initiatives.
